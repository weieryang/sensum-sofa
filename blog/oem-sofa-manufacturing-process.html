OEM Sofa Manufacturing Process for Importers

A step-by-step guide for furniture buyers planning private label sofa projects.

Send InquiryContact Sales

Step-by-Step OEM Process

  1. Requirement confirmation: target market, product style, dimensions, price range and order quantity.
  2. Material selection: fabric, foam, frame, accessories, legs and packaging.
  3. Sample preparation: confirm design, comfort, appearance and packing method.
  4. Quotation: calculate price based on quantity, materials and packing.
  5. Bulk production: coordinate production, inspection, label and shipment details.

How to Speed Up Quotation

Send reference photos, target size, destination country, order quantity, fabric preference and target price. This helps the supplier recommend a realistic model and packing plan.
