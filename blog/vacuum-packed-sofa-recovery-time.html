What B2B sofa buyers should check before ordering vacuum packed or compressed sofas.

Send InquiryContact Sales

What Affects Recovery?

Recovery time depends on foam density, foam resilience, product structure, temperature, humidity and how long the product has been stored in compressed condition. Buyers should check recovery performance using the exact sample and packing method.

Recommended Buyer Checks

Sample Recovery

Unpack a sample and record the recovery condition at different time points.

Storage Simulation

Ask whether recovery has been checked after storage time similar to your shipping cycle.

Customer Instruction

Prepare clear unpacking and recovery instructions for end users.
