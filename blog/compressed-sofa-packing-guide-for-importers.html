Why packing data matters before price negotiation

For B2B furniture importers, the lowest unit price is not always the best total cost. A compressed sofa with a clear carton size and CBM reference helps buyers estimate freight, warehouse handling, local delivery and container usage before placing an order.

When comparing compressed sofa suppliers, buyers should not only ask for model photos and FOB price. Packing details are part of the real landing-cost calculation.

Buyer tip: Ask suppliers for carton size, CBM, gross weight and 40HQ loading reference together with the quotation. This keeps communication faster and avoids repeated back-and-forth.

1. Carton size: the first number warehouse teams check

Carton size affects warehouse handling, delivery channel selection and storage planning. For compressed sofa products, a smaller carton can make it easier for distributors and online furniture sellers to manage stock and local delivery.

2. CBM per unit: the key to freight cost comparison

CBM per unit is one of the most important numbers for importers. It helps buyers estimate how much space each sofa uses in shipping and how freight cost is distributed across each unit.

When two models have a similar unit price, the model with lower CBM may offer better landed-cost performance for cross-border sellers and wholesale buyers.

3. 40HQ loading reference: useful, but not a fixed promise

A 40HQ loading reference helps buyers estimate container planning before negotiation. However, it should be treated as a reference instead of an absolute guarantee, because actual loading depends on carton layout, model mix, packaging method and final order structure.

For buyers importing several models together, it is better to ask for a mixed-container loading estimate after confirming model numbers and quantities.

FAQ

Why do compressed sofas reduce shipping pressure?

Vacuum or compressed packing can reduce carton volume compared with normal assembled furniture, helping buyers improve container usage and warehouse handling.

Can carton size be customized?

Some packaging details can be discussed depending on model, order quantity and OEM requirements. Buyers should confirm carton marking, label and packaging needs before production.

Should I choose the model with the lowest CBM?

Not always. Buyers should consider comfort, market positioning, product size, retail price, delivery method and total landed cost together.
