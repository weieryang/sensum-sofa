Vacuum Compression and Carton Packing

For compressed sofa programs, packing design is a major part of the purchasing decision. A good packing plan can reduce shipping volume, make handling easier and improve the buyer's warehousing efficiency.

Vacuum Compression

Foam-based parts can be compressed according to model structure and material condition.

Carton Packing

Carton size, weight and protection are planned for container loading and retail handling.

Recovery Check

Recovery performance should be checked after unpacking to confirm the selling condition.

What Buyers Should Confirm

  • Final carton size and gross weight
  • Container loading quantity for 20GP / 40HQ
  • Storage time before unpacking
  • Foam density and recovery expectation
  • Retail handling and warehouse requirements

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Weieryang support OEM and ODM sofa projects?

Yes. Weieryang supports OEM and ODM projects, including size adjustment, fabric selection, color matching, carton artwork, packing plan and product series development for B2B buyers.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Please send model number, order quantity, destination country, preferred fabric, packing requirement and target price. The sales team can then calculate a more accurate bulk quotation.

Who are the main buyers for compressed sofas?

Compressed sofas are suitable for furniture importers, wholesalers, online furniture brands, chain retailers, distributors and project buyers that need space-saving packing and container efficiency.

Does compression affect sofa recovery?

Compression should be matched with the right foam, structure and packing time. Buyers should confirm recovery testing for the selected model before bulk orders.

Can carton size be customized?

Carton size may be adjusted depending on product dimensions, compression method, protection requirement and container loading target.
