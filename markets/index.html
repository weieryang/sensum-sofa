One sofa program, different market questions

The same model can require a different project file when the destination, retailer or fulfillment method changes. Market planning does not mean changing a country name on the same page. It means identifying what must be confirmed for that buyer before the sample and carton artwork are approved.

Product and channel

Define the retail price band, room size, seat configuration, online or store channel, delivery method and customer assembly expectation.

Documentation scope

List the reports, labels, declarations, product information and shipment files the importer expects for the exact SKU and destination.

Packing and fulfillment

Confirm carton dimensions, package count, gross weight, barcode rules, warehouse limits and damage-evidence workflow before loading.

Market planning FAQ

Why should the destination be included in the first RFQ?

The destination affects the document scope, labels, carton marks, fulfillment constraints and product information that should be reviewed before production.

Can one compliance file be used for every sofa and country?

No. Match each file to the ordered SKU, materials, destination and current requirement. The importer should confirm the final scope with qualified local professionals.

Can market-specific orders include private-label packaging?

Private-label projects can discuss carton artwork, product labels, barcodes, instructions and product information, subject to the model, quantity and confirmed destination requirements.

Does this page replace an importer compliance review?

No. It is a procurement framework. The importer remains responsible for confirming current legal, customs, retailer and marketplace requirements before ordering or selling.
