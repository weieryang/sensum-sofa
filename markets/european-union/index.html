Compressed sofa sourcing for EU importers

Updated: 2026-09-19 · Market: EU Member States · Audience: importers, furniture brands and online retailers

Choose the destination country before approving the sofa. A useful EU sourcing file connects the exact model and materials to the importer, product-safety review, consumer information, carton data and final sales channel.

Scope note: This is a procurement checklist, not legal advice or a claim that a listed model is EU compliant. The importer should verify the current rules for the complete SKU, intended use, Member State, sales channel and packaging with qualified local advisers before ordering or selling. The United Kingdom is not an EU Member State and needs a separate review.

Name the Member State and the sales channel first

“Europe” is not enough for a production brief. Identify the first EU country where the sofa will be sold, who imports it, whether it goes to a retailer or directly to consumers, and whether one sellable unit travels in one or several cartons. A second country can change the language, marketplace content, packaging reporting and customer-service plan. Avoid printing final artwork until these decisions are fixed.

Shortlist the compressed sofa family by actual seat configuration and dimensions. Then ask for a sample of the intended fabric, filling, frame, legs and packed configuration. A document for a related model is not proof for the order you are placing.

Build a product-safety file around the exact sofa

The EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) has applied since 13 December 2024 to consumer non-food products within its scope. It addresses product safety and traceability, including online and distance sales. The importer should identify the applicable economic-operator roles and review the manufacturer details, product identification, risk information, supporting documents and a way to handle safety issues. Keep the approved product revision visible on every file.

For a compressed sofa, an internal buyer file can include the bill of materials, assembly and unpacking instructions, dimensions, component count, sample photos, material evidence, inspection criteria and carton artwork. This is a practical handover pack, not a substitute for any legally required technical documentation or a product-specific safety assessment.

Do not use CE marking as a generic sofa badge

EU guidance is explicit: a CE mark is required only when specific EU harmonised rules require it for that product; where no such rule applies, it must not be used. Ordinary upholstered seating should not be described as “CE certified” by default. If a sofa includes an electrical recliner, power supply, charging port or other function, the importer must assess the complete configuration and any applicable product-specific rules separately.

Our document library is an inventory of available files, not blanket market approval. Ask which model, material, component, test method and report date each file actually covers. Do not add a mark, declaration or certificate reference to carton artwork until the importer's product-scope review is complete.

Request material evidence, not a generic “REACH certificate”

For upholstery, foam, adhesives, finishes and components, the buyer should ask for a material list and the evidence needed for its chemical-compliance review. Under REACH Article 33, suppliers of articles containing a Candidate List substance above 0.1% by weight must communicate sufficient safe-use information to recipients. The importer should evaluate article-level information and any SCIP duties for the actual construction rather than treating one generic test sheet as covering every fabric and component.

When an approved fabric or filling changes after sampling, reopen the material and document review. Record the new component reference and determine whether existing evidence remains relevant before authorising bulk production.

Approve country-language information and online offers

Where instructions and safety information are needed, the GPSR guidance says they must be in a language easily understood by consumers, as determined by the Member State where the product is offered. This is why an English-only insert cannot be assumed sufficient for every EU destination. Have the importer approve the language set, safety wording, product identifiers and operator details before printing.

For online or distance sales, the importer should also review the visible offer information required by the applicable rules, not just the physical carton. Give the retail team consistent product photographs, dimensions, package count and assembly information from the same approved SKU file.

Treat packaging as both a fulfillment and country-responsibility decision

Request carton dimensions, gross weight, package count per sellable unit, CBM, protection layers, unpacking sequence and a loading estimate for the actual SKU mix. These numbers affect warehouse receiving, delivery charges and damage claims. A vacuum-packed sofa also needs a sample recovery check after opening, not only a compressed-size claim.

EU packaging rules and extended producer responsibility (EPR) require a separate importer review. The responsible company and its registration or reporting obligations depend on how and where packaging is placed on the market. Identify the destination Member State, seller and packaging materials before final artwork and shipment. The factory can provide a packaging bill of materials and weight data; the buyer determines the applicable obligations.

EU importer FAQ

Do all EU sofas need a CE mark?

No. CE marking is required only when the exact product falls under EU legislation mandating it. Assess electrical functions or other regulated features separately; do not mark ordinary seating by default.

Can the same English instruction sheet be used in every EU country?

Do not assume so. Where instructions and safety information are needed, their language must be easily understood by consumers in the destination Member State. Confirm the language set before printing.

What should an EU importer include in an RFQ?

Send the destination country, importer and sales channel, exact model and materials, quantity, carton limits, required languages, artwork, product-safety document request and shipment timing.
