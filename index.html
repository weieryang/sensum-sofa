Compressed Sofa FAQ for B2B Buyers

These answers help importers, distributors and wholesalers understand compressed sofa sourcing before requesting a quotation.

What is a compressed sofa?

A compressed sofa is a foam sofa that is vacuum packed into a smaller carton to reduce shipping volume and improve container loading efficiency.

Can I customize fabric, color and carton?

Yes. Weieryang supports OEM / ODM fabric, color, size, carton, label and packing customization based on buyer requirements.

How long does shape recovery take?

Most compressed sofas recover after unpacking, and recovery time can vary based on foam density, temperature and packaging duration.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Please send model number, quantity, destination country, preferred fabric/color, target price and packaging requirements.
