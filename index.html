Production flow

From raw foam to export-ready cartons.

Each order follows a defined production sequence, with specifications checked from material preparation through final packing.

  1. 01
    Material preparation

    Foam and upholstery materials are selected and prepared to match the approved specification.

  2. 02
    Cutting and shaping

    Foam and fabric components are cut to the approved profiles and dimensions.

  3. 03
    Sewing and upholstery

    Covers and upholstered components are sewn and fitted to the specified construction.

  4. 04
    Assembly and inspection

    Finished pieces are checked for dimensions, appearance, comfort and workmanship.

  5. 05
    Compression and packing

    Approved sofas are vacuum-compressed, packed, labeled and prepared for shipment.
