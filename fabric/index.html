Frequently Asked Questions

Can Weieryang support OEM and ODM sofa projects?

Yes. Weieryang supports OEM and ODM projects, including size adjustment, fabric selection, color matching, carton artwork, packing plan and product series development for B2B buyers.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Please send model number, order quantity, destination country, preferred fabric, packing requirement and target price. The sales team can then calculate a more accurate bulk quotation.

Who are the main buyers for compressed sofas?

Compressed sofas are suitable for furniture importers, wholesalers, online furniture brands, chain retailers, distributors and project buyers that need space-saving packing and container efficiency.

Can buyers choose sofa fabric color?

Yes. Buyers can choose available colors or discuss custom color matching based on project quantity and fabric availability.

Can one fabric be used across several sofa models?

Yes. A shared fabric system can help buyers create a consistent product series and simplify inventory planning.
