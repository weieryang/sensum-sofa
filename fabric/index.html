Fabric, color and upholstery-planning guidance for importers building compressed sofa, sofa bed and modular sofa collections.

Fabric choice affects more than product appearance

On compressed sofa projects, fabric selection influences product positioning, sample approval, MOQ planning, private-label consistency and how the sofa looks after unpacking. Buyers who decide the upholstery route early usually get clearer quotations and fewer revisions than buyers who treat fabric as a late-stage color choice.

Market Fit

Choose a surface direction that matches the target channel, price band and buyer expectation for texture and finish.

Color Control

Plan core colors and optional colors together so MOQ and repeat-order logic stay manageable.

Sample Accuracy

Approve the fabric on the real sample so seam appearance, recovery behavior and visual proportion are visible before bulk release.

Frequently Asked Questions

How should buyers choose sofa fabric for a compressed sofa project?

Buyers should choose fabric based on target market, texture direction, color plan, MOQ impact, sample appearance and whether the material still fits the packing and recovery workflow of the selected model.

Can one fabric be used across several sofa models?

Yes. A shared fabric system can help create a coherent product family, but buyers should still confirm seam appearance, recovery behavior and MOQ by SKU before final approval.

Does fabric selection affect quotation or lead time?

Yes. Fabric choice can affect booking quantity, color availability, sample timing, MOQ and whether the order stays within the planned launch window.

What should be recorded in the final fabric approval file?

The final approval file should record the fabric code, color direction, approved sample photos, related models, packing notes and any private-label or labeling requirements tied to that fabric route.

Which buyer guides should be reviewed with the fabric page?

Buyers should review the sample approval checklist, MOQ and lead-time guide, private-label guide and OEM / ODM page when the fabric route is part of a larger compressed sofa sourcing project.
