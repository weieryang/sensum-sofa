How to measure your window seat cushion

Measure the usable seating surface, then record the cushion's finished thickness separately. A rectangle needs length and depth; an angled bay window also needs the front and back widths, side measurements and a labeled sketch. Send those dimensions with your preferred design so the finished size can be agreed before production.

Which part of the window seat should I measure?

Measure the surface that will actually support the cushion, rather than the wall-to-wall opening or the outside of the cabinet. Check the front, middle and back: walls, trim and bench edges may not be parallel. Note any change instead of averaging it into one length.

Record the space available beneath handles, opening windows and drawers. Describe whether the cushion should fit inside an opening or overhang the bench edge. Do not subtract a universal fitting allowance yourself; agree the finished dimensions and any clearance with the seller.

What measurements does an angled bay window need?

Label the front edge, back edge, left side and right side on a top-view sketch. Include the perpendicular seat depth and any corner cutouts. Side lengths alone do not fully describe every irregular shape, so add diagonal measurements or a template when the shape cannot be identified from the sketch.

A photo helps explain the orientation, but does not replace measured dimensions. Mark where the window and room face are. For a curved seat or several separate cushions, describe the curve or each segment and agree the required template before ordering.

Is cushion thickness the same as the decorative front drop?

No. Finished thickness is the height of the seating cushion. A front drop is the fabric section that hangs below the front edge; it is a separate dimension and does not describe the foam thickness. If you want a front drop, send its height and say which edges it should cover.

Choose the thickness in relation to the bench height and available clearance. The reference photos do not establish foam density or firmness. Ask the seller to confirm the foam specification, finished thickness and construction for your order.

Should I send inches or centimeters?

Either is fine, as long as every dimension is labeled with the same unit. The exact conversion is 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters. For example, a rectangular size of 48 × 20 × 3 inches converts to 121.92 × 50.80 × 7.62 centimeters.

That is a measurement example, not a stocked size or manufacturing tolerance. Keep your original measurements in the inquiry; confirm the final unit, rounding and finished dimensions on the order specification.

What should I send to receive a cushion quotation?

Send the design reference, labeled dimensions and unit, quantity, destination country and postal code. Add your color preference, front-drop dimensions and any shape sketch. The form below prepares this information for email or WhatsApp.

Before approving the order, check the agreed finished size, fabric reference and composition, foam specification, cover construction, item price, shipping charge and production time. If you need a particular care method, fastening or accessory, include it explicitly.
