How do teddy, corduroy and woven window seat cushion designs differ?

WY-WC01 shows a cream teddy-style surface with rounded stitched channels and a front drop. WY-WC02 shows ribbed corduroy-style references in ivory and grey. WY-WC03 has a grey, understated linen-like woven appearance. WY-WC04 uses a mustard reference with a more visible woven texture.

These are observations of the supplied reference photos. Choose the texture and color direction that suits your room, then open the corresponding product page for the details to confirm. Availability of the exact fabric and final color is checked in the quotation.

Does cotton-linen style mean a confirmed cotton and linen blend?

No. In this catalog, cotton-linen style describes the appearance. It does not state a tested fiber percentage. Likewise, a teddy or corduroy appearance does not identify the fiber blend. Ask for the composition and fabric reference of the material selected for your order.

If a particular fiber content or performance requirement matters, include it in the inquiry. Request supporting information for that exact fabric rather than assuming that a sofa certificate or another material's report covers the cushion.

How do I check whether the cover is removable or washable?

Confirm the cover construction and the care instructions separately. A removable cover may have a zipper or another opening, but removal alone does not establish machine-washability. Ask which parts can be removed and how the selected fabric and finished cover should be cleaned.

If you need a replacement cover, anti-slip backing, ties or another fastening, list it in the quotation request. Do not assume these features are included because similar cushions elsewhere have them.

How should I confirm the color and fabric direction?

Use the photo to describe the intended color family, then agree the actual fabric reference. Lighting and screens can change how a shade appears. If close color matching is important, ask whether a physical swatch or another agreed reference is available; availability and any sample costs require confirmation.

For corduroy, specify the rib direction across the seat. For a long cushion or multiple segments, confirm seam placement and the layout across the whole bench. For teddy channels, confirm channel orientation and the front-edge treatment separately from cushion thickness.

Can I compare the price using only these photos?

A fair comparison needs the same finished dimensions, shape, thickness, foam specification, fabric, cover construction and quantity. Compare the item price and shipping charge separately for the same destination. A low price for a different size or different construction is not a like-for-like quote.

There are no confirmed online prices for these four photo-based designs yet. Send the reference and your requirements using the inquiry form. The quotation should identify what is included and confirm production time before payment.
