Design & specification

A ribbed texture in two reference colorways

Corduroy creates a visible ribbed pattern that changes with the direction of the fabric and the light. Start with the ivory reference for a light palette, or compare the grey reference with darker cabinetry. For a long window bench, agree the rib direction and panel layout before production. These details help keep the appearance consistent across the full length of the seat.

Sizing guide

How to measure a custom window seat cushion

  1. Measure the seating area

    Measure the usable length and depth on the bench, in inches or centimeters. For a bay window, record front and back widths plus angled edges; include a sketch or template.

  2. Separate thickness and drop

    Choose the finished cushion thickness separately from any decorative front drop. Check the height against windows, handles, drawers and the intended sitting position.

  3. Confirm the complete quote

    Send your preferred photo, color, quantity and postal code. Confirm fabric, foam, price, shipping and production time before approving the order.

Window seat cushion questions

Can I choose grey instead of the ivory reference?

Yes, grey is a second reference option in this family. Include your preferred photo and catalog reference WY-WC02 in the inquiry; fabric availability and the final shade are confirmed in the quotation.

Can I request a standard size or a custom size?

Both can be discussed. Send your required dimensions. Standard-size stock and final custom specifications must be confirmed in the quotation; the photo is not a size or stock guarantee.

What determines the cushion price?

The quoted specification includes length, depth, thickness, shape, fabric, foam, front edge and quantity. Shipping is checked separately for the destination. A photo design is not priced by the sofa packing data or a generic cushion estimate.

Is the cover removable, washable or suitable for outdoors?

These features are not established by the reference photos. Ask for the selected fabric composition, care instructions and cover construction. This catalog presents indoor seating references and does not claim waterproofing or outdoor suitability.

How are delivery and payment confirmed?

Include the destination country and postal code with your inquiry. The seller confirms the shipping option, charge, estimated schedule and available payment method for that order. This page is an inquiry catalog, not a live payment checkout.
