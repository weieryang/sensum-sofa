Products and factory scope

What types of compressed sofas do you manufacture?

Weieryang manufactures compressed sofas, sofa beds, modular sofas and foam-based upholstered furniture for importers, retailers, furniture brands and e-commerce sellers. The production system covers foam preparation, cutting, upholstery, assembly, vacuum compression, packing and export loading support.

Why choose Weieryang for compressed sofa sourcing?

Weieryang integrates foam processing, upholstery, assembly, vacuum compression and export packing under one factory system. This helps buyers coordinate product development, quality control, packing efficiency and shipment preparation with one accountable manufacturing team.

OEM, ODM and MOQ

Do you support OEM and ODM sofa production?

Yes. Weieryang supports OEM and ODM compressed sofa production, including custom dimensions, fabric selection, color options, logo labels, carton design, packaging requirements and model development based on buyer reference samples or target market needs.

What is the MOQ for compressed sofa orders?

MOQ depends on the sofa model, fabric selection, customization level and packing requirements. For standard models, Weieryang can review trial orders with importers and retailers before moving to larger bulk production.

Can you customize fabric, size and sofa construction?

Yes. Fabric, color, size, foam profile, upholstery details, cover construction, carton size, label and packaging information can be discussed according to the buyer's market and order requirements. See the OEM/ODM page and fabric options for related planning details.

Vacuum packing and recovery

How long can a sofa stay vacuum compressed?

The suitable compression period depends on foam density, sofa structure, packing method and shipping conditions. Weieryang evaluates compression recovery and packing stability by model and provides recommended packing and storage guidance before shipment.

How well does the sofa recover after unpacking?

Compressed sofa recovery depends on foam specification, construction and compression duration. Weieryang checks dimensions, appearance, comfort and recovery performance during product development and before bulk shipment.

How many compressed sofas can fit in a 40HQ container?

The 40HQ loading quantity depends on the model size, compressed carton size and packing method. Weieryang provides model-specific CBM and container loading estimates to help importers compare shipping efficiency before order confirmation. Review the packing and loading page for more context.

Quality control and samples

Do you provide sample orders before bulk production?

Yes. Sample orders can be arranged for model review, comfort testing, fabric confirmation, compression recovery check and packaging evaluation before bulk production.

What quality checks are done before packing?

Quality checks include material specification, foam cutting, sewing details, dimensions, appearance, assembly quality, comfort, compression recovery, carton marking and packing data before shipment.

Can you support Amazon, retailer or importer packaging requirements?

Weieryang can discuss carton labeling, barcode labels, private label packaging, carton size, product information and packing documents based on the buyer's sales channel and destination market.

Quotation preparation

What information should buyers send for quotation?

Buyers can send the target sofa model, reference photos, dimensions, fabric requirements, order quantity, packaging requirements, destination country and expected delivery schedule. Weieryang will review the product and provide model, MOQ, packing and loading suggestions.

Where should buyers start?

Start by shortlisting models in the product catalog, then send the model number, target market and order requirements through contact or WhatsApp. If you are still comparing suppliers, read the compressed sofa manufacturer checklist.
